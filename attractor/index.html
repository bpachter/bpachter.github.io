You are moving at 600 km/s toward something we've never seen.

Right now — you, the Earth, the Sun, the entire Milky Way — are streaming through space at about 600 kilometers every second, toward a point in the sky hidden behind our own galaxy. This is the story of your real address: the giant hole you live in, the web outside it, the two forces fighting over everything, and the mass that is quietly reeling us in.
