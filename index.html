02

Thessa

Supply-chain knowledge graph
Ontology designReact + TypeScriptSEC EDGAR · USAspendingProvenance-firstMIT
A knowledge graph of who depends on whom across space, defense, AI infrastructure and critical minerals — 39,606 typed nodes across 10 types — 15,048 of them organizations, the rest programs, products, segments, people and materials — joined by 50,124 typed edges across 20 relation kinds, and 78,226 federal awards worth $1.98T, of which 97.5% resolve to a recipient already in the graph. The hard part isn't the join, it's deciding what earns a place: a filer must name any customer above 10% of revenue and state the dollar figure, so that edge carries an audited number, while a press release carries only an assertion. They are stored on different trust rungs — 2,803 edges from filings and federal records against 1,187 press-derived — and refuted claims stay in the audit ledger rather than quietly disappearing. Free, no paid tier, open source.
