02 · Ethos

I learned to read the whole board before making the first move.

I've been a strategist since before I had the word for it — running Minecraft servers and writing mods before that was a thing kids did, losing weekends to Civilization and Age of Empires, always more interested in the system underneath the game than the game itself.

I set out for military intelligence and started down the ROTC path in college. Early on I was diagnosed with Crohn's disease, which closed the door on service. It was a hard reroute — but the instinct that pulled me toward intelligence work (read a complex system, find the leverage point, think several moves ahead) didn't go anywhere.

I've spent my career aiming that instinct at hard problems: building the data platforms and AI systems that turn ambiguous business questions into decisions. I tend to operate like a pseudo-entrepreneur inside a larger organization — high agency, high ownership, comfortable holding the whole picture from data pipeline to boardroom. Same strategy game.
